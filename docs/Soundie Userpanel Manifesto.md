# SOUNDIE USER PANEL MANIFEST  
## The Sanctuary Dashboard

---

# Core Principle

The user panel is not an admin dashboard.

It is not productivity software.  
It is not analytics overload.

It is a mirror of inner rhythm.

When the user opens their panel, they should feel:

“I am returning to myself.”

---

# Purpose of the User Panel

The panel should help users:

- see their listening journey
- understand emotional patterns
- reconnect with favorite notes
- feel progress without pressure
- receive gentle guidance
- return to calm quickly

---

# Emotional UX Rule

Every metric must feel compassionate.

Never:

- guilt
- streak shame
- missed-day pressure
- red warning energy
- “you failed”

Always:

- continuity
- invitation
- softness
- momentum
- renewal

---

# Main Dashboard Layout

## 1. Welcome Header

Examples:

Good evening, your notes remember you.  
You’ve been seeking clarity lately.  
Stillness is available now.  
Welcome back to your inner weather.

Include:

- time of day greeting
- subtle emotional insight
- one CTA

CTA:

Begin Session

---

## 2. Today State Card

Shows:

- mood check-in (optional)
- minutes listened today
- current dominant note
- suggested next note

Example:

Today you’ve spent 9 minutes with The Beacon.  
Suggested now: The Keeper.

---

## 3. Journey Overview

Not stats-heavy.

Display:

- total sessions
- total minutes
- notes awakened
- favorite note
- current streak (optional, soft language)

Instead of “streak” use:

Rhythm: 4 days connected

---

## 4. Collection Garden

Visual map of all 12 notes.

States:

- sleeping
- discovered
- bonded
- awakened
- mastered

Tap opens note profile.

---

## 5. Emotional Patterns

Weekly reflection:

You visited grounding tones often this week.  
You may be seeking stability.  

or

Dream notes increased.  
Your mind may be processing change.

---

## 6. AI Guidance Card

Examples:

Try 5 minutes with The River before work.  
The Dreamer may help tonight.  
You’ve avoided rest lately. Sit with C.

---

## 7. Sacred Archive

History of sessions:

Apr 26 — The Beacon — 5 min  
Apr 25 — The Foundation — 8 min

Include notes felt after session.

# Premium Panel Additions

- nervous system trends
- custom journeys

- deeper lore unlocks
- monthly reflection letters

---

# Design Language

Minimal. Spacious. Warm.

Should feel like:

- opening a linen journal
- entering a quiet chapel
- checking weather inside yourself

---

# Colors

Neutral base.

Accent colors come from current dominant note.

If user listened to A most:

panel glows blue subtly.

---

# Microcopy Rules

Use:

Welcome back.  
Begin gently.  
You are already enough.  
Another minute matters.  
Return when ready.

Avoid:

You missed yesterday  
Increase productivity  
Optimize performance  
Stay ahead

---

# Data to Track Quietly

- listen minutes
- repeat notes
- time of day habits
- session completion
- emotional tags
- return frequency
- combinations explored

Use insights, not pressure.

---

# Final Law

The panel should never feel like homework.

It should feel like coming home.
