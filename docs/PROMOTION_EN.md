### 🌱 What is Soundie?

Soundie is something that didn't exist before.

Not a meditation app. Not a music game. Not a focus timer.

It's a **living world of sound** — 12 notes, each with its own soul, its own ancient story, and its own effect on your nervous system. You listen to them. They grow. And in return — **something in you settles**.

---

### 🎮 How does it work?

```
Choose a note → Listen → The note grows → Lore is revealed
```

Each session is one repetition of a ritual:

- 🔊 **Sound generated in real time** — Web Audio API, no MP3 files, no loops
- 📖 **5 lore layers** per note — unlocked through listening time, not payments
- 🃏 **The Teardrop Deck** — artifact cards tied to each note's emotional tone, earned through practice
- 🌊 **Echo** — your mirror: time spent with each emotional climate, mood patterns, Teardrop path
- 🌳 **Tree** — your living garden of notes

There's no rush. The stiller you sit — the more is revealed.

### 🔮 Teardrop — the card system

Every listening session edges you closer to cards from the **Teardrop Deck** — a layered collection tied to phases of the inner journey:

```
🌱 Roots → 🌊 Flow → 🌑 Void → ✨ Light → 🧙 Archetypes
```

Each card is not a reward. It's a **mirror** — showing you what you just moved through while listening.

Cards like *The Vessel*, *The Spiral*, *The Return*, *The Lightkeeper* surface at the right moment. They carry taglines, affirmations, shadow readings. They're not fortune-telling. They're reflection.

---

### 💡 Why Soundie is different from everything else

> *"The app does not demand your attention. It rewards your stillness."*

- ❌ No "come back or lose your streak" notifications
- ❌ No energy bar that depletes overnight
- ❌ No ads hidden behind a pause button
- ❌ No "unlock the truth with Premium"

Notes unlock through **listening** — not paying.

The longer you stay, the more you receive. That's the only mechanic that matters.

---

### 🌿 The world underneath

Soundie exists in a world almost identical to ours — same cities, same oceans, same history. But hidden beneath ordinary civilization is an older layer of reality: **Resonance**.

Ancient cultures discovered that tones, intervals, and harmonic relationships affect biology, emotion, memory, and states of consciousness. Most of this knowledge was fragmented, buried, or forgotten.

Soundie is the rediscovery.

Players don't collect monsters. They **awaken Acoustic Archetypes** — living entities formed where frequency and human attention meet.
